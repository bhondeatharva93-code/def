Returns and refunds

If something is wrong with your order, tell us within 7 days of delivery. Keep the original box and packing until we have sorted it out.

  • Items that arrive damaged or not as described are replaced or refunded.

Every part carries the manufacturer's warranty. The period is listed on the product page and on your invoice.

  • Keep your GST invoice. You need it to claim warranty.
