ABOUT US

Welcome to Computer Galaxy

Computer Galaxy is a GST-registered computer sales, custom PC building and computer service business located in Sirohi, Rajasthan, India.

We specialize in custom-built computers, gaming PCs, laptops, professional workstations, computer hardware, accessories, repair services and system upgrades.

Our goal is to provide customers with genuine computer products, reliable performance, competitive prices and professional technical guidance.

We understand that every customer has different requirements. Therefore, we offer customized computer configurations based on individual budgets, applications and performance needs.

Every custom-built PC is professionally assembled and tested before dispatch.

Through our official technology brand, WeblinkitTech, we share computer knowledge, technology tips, PC buying guides, product reviews and custom PC build videos on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.
